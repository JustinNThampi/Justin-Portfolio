Healthcare Appointment App

A patient-focused app for scheduling healthcare appointments, tracking bookings, and managing care communication in a clean mobile interface.

Play Store link coming soon Back to Portfolio

Overview

The app helps users find doctors, confirm available appointment slots, and keep their healthcare schedule up to date with notifications and a simple booking flow.

Problem Solved

Booking appointments manually or through fragmented systems can be confusing and error-prone. This app streamlines appointment management in a single mobile experience.

Key Features

  • Doctor profile browsing with speciality filters
  • Appointment booking and confirmation flow
  • Reminders and status updates for upcoming visits
  • Patient-focused booking history and rescheduling

What I Built

I designed smooth and readable Flutter screens, connected appointment flows to backend data, and built a notification-ready layout for managing patient appointments.

  • Created search and filter interfaces for providers
  • Integrated appointment booking with offline-safe state handling
  • Added patient history tracking for completed and upcoming visits
  • Refined UI interactions for accessibility and ease of use
