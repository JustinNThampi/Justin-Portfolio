Overview

This app delivers an intuitive mobile storefront with product browsing, cart previews, and checkout readiness for users on the go.

Problem Solved

Many shopping apps overcomplicate buying journeys. This project focuses on a cleaner product experience, easier browsing, and a more straightforward checkout process.

Key Features

  • Featured product browsing with categories and filters
  • Shopping cart support with quantity and price updates
  • Responsive checkout flow with validation hints
  • Fast animations and product-image transitions

What I Built

I implemented the mobile shopping experience in Flutter, built intuitive product controls, and optimized the app to keep interactions fluid and engaging.

  • Created product card components and category filters
  • Built cart and order summary screens with state management
  • Added responsive spacing and consistent brand styling
  • Focused on improving load time through efficient list rendering
